# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Give and gracefully accept useful feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.
- Respect privacy, copyright, and security boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private information, and publishing copyrighted books or credentials are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile or a private repository security channel. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
